### The Problem

Some community members correctly voiced the concern that with how the current Rarity Score is set with the 7 main categories (body, clothing, hair, eyes, nose, mouth, facial hair), a female Flovatar would either have to give up on getting the highest rarity score or be forced to choose a facial hair trait. While it is impossible to build a completely fair and equal ecosystem for everyone, we strongly believed this was a significant issue that needed to be addressed, not by just an internal decision but also with broader community participation.

### Community opinions

We started to bring out the issue to the community because we think that if we are really aiming to be a proper DAO one day, this would be a very good first example of one of the many problems that the DAO will have to face and eventually solve in the future. That is because this problem involves a technical component and requires broad social consensus from multiple parties holding differing views.

We asked if we should just simply remove that category from the rarity score calculation, and the majority of the community decided it would be a fair thing to do so with 357 people voting yes and 52 voting no.

So the quick and easy solution might have been to just proceed with that plan, but we think a DAO has to be so much more than just a simple binary yes/no decision and should include the opinions and concerns of everyone. A true resolution lies in also understanding the nuances from each party.

For that reason we will always be very very careful in making any decision if that would have a long-standing impact on the community. We don’t think we should expect a 99% agreement before making a final decision because it would make us become way too static, but we definitely should always aim so that 99% of people would understand the rationale and thought process that went behind any decision made.

You don’t need to always like it or agree with it (just because the majority did so) but we will ensure you understand it and don’t feel left out.

After hearing out all the very reasonable and understandable concerns made especially by those of you who already used their Boosters on facial hair traits to mint a Flovatar, it was obvious very soon that we already reached a point where making any change would have serious implications on quite a considerable amount of Flovatars, and we simply don’t want this to happen either. Changing rules on the go is never something that should be done lightly and with just a normal consensus. It’s always better if we can have stability, but it doesn’t mean that we have to remain blocked on some rule just because they happened to be decided at the origin of the project.

For this reason we decided to not stop there and try to look at other potential alternatives and solutions.

### Solomonic proposal

After a lot of thinking and rethinking we came with what we hope is the most balanced and fair solution to this problem, and we would love to hear your reactions and comments.

The idea is simple and we feel it could address concerns from two major conflicting camps. It is to change the Rarity Score calculation using only the 6 highest rarity traits out of the 7 available for each Flovatars. What it means in practice is that any Flovatar with 6 or less boosters would receive a small rarity score increase because now the denominator is 6 instead of 7 categories, the only exception are the Flovatars with zero rarity boosters, they will still remain at zero score. However, if a Flovatar had 7 boosters, the new update would consider the 6 rarest traits to derive the final rarity score, with 6 Legendary traits giving 100 score.

How would this address concerns from each camp?

**Camp 1: “Female Flovatars cannot reach maximum score without adding the facial hair feature”**

Since we will be taking into consideration only 6 out 7 rarest traits used for a Flovatar, now a 6 legendary trait Flovatar will have a 100 rarity score (the max). This should make it fair for all female Flovatar minted, including when it comes to future airdrop considerations.

**Camp 2: “We already minted Flovatars with facial hair and spent valuable boosters on them”**

With this new change, Flovatars that have a facial hair trait and used less than or equal to 6 boosters would not be affected at all. That is because all 6 traits will be used to calculate the final rarity score. Only Flovatars who have used all 7 boosters will be affected, but we have a solution for that below.

We ran a blockchain query to see how many Flovatars had used all 7 rarity boosters. To date, there were a total of 149 Flovatars matching that criteria. The good news is that none of them will be losing their current rarity score, it actually can only stay the same or even go up, because we will be taking their highest 6 traits. For example, if someone had a 7 epic boosters Flovatar, they originally would have had a total score of 19.355. With the new model, they would still keep the same score. On the contrary, if someone had a 1 rare and 6 epic boosters Flovatar, their original score was 17.051 while the new one would be 19.355.

That said, we are aware that in the case of 7 boosters Flovatars, the main downside of the new change would be the feeling that they have wasted one extra booster, since they could have used 6 instead of 7. To address that one remaining issue, we will airdrop to each of the 149 Flovatars a rarity booster matching the lowest one they used, given that one would be excluded from the rarity score calculation.

### Failed ideas

The first one was, like described at the beginning, to simply discard the facial hair traits from the calculation of the Rarity Score, but also to compensate the ones that used their Boosters with their facial traits. This would have been a bit expensive but still doable on our end. The problem in this case is that If someone had concentrated all his boosters into one Flovatar, the compensation of having one booster back wouldn’t be good enough because he wouldn’t be able to create a new one with equal or higher Rarity Score. So for this reason we decided to discard this option.

The second solution we came up is to just keep things like they are for the sake of continuity and consistency, but at the same time to compensate all the female Flovatars (that could be identified by a specific subset of traits) with a special Flobit airdrop aimed just for them and with a limited edition. But this solution felt also half-baked and wouldn’t solve the main issue in the long run and would open questions to the real value of the compensation given in comparison to the drawbacks that are pre-existing.

We also considered including the Flobits in the Rarity Score, but that would open up to a whole series of other complications especially at the time of taking snapshots to distribute DUST or Airdrops. Since Flobits can be swapped any time, it would be pretty likely to happen that lots of people would forget to put on all their rarest Flobits to optimize their score. For this reason this option has been ruled out as well.

### Final notes

We strongly feel that this could be a very fair way to rebalance things. At the same time the impact on Flovatars that have used 7 rare traits would be very much contained and we would be able to compensate them fairly for the original oversight that we made by not making the Rarity Score script this way from the very beginning.

Once we feel ready, we will open a dedicated channel on Discord called “simple-dao-prop-1” so that we can keep the conversation going and ultimately come to a final decision as a proper DAO is supposed to do.

Please feel free to express your opinion and to share your point of view on this. Each one of them is worth hearing and understanding before taking any decision.

Thanks again and sorry for the super long message but I wanted to show you that we are not taking this lightly and I am really working hard on looking for the best possible solution to this.
